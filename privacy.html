Camera

When you scan your cube, the app uses the camera to read the colours of the stickers. The camera picture is analysed briefly in memory for that purpose. It is never saved as a photo or video and never leaves your iPhone. You can refuse camera access and enter the colours by hand instead.

What the app stores on your iPhone

  • the history of your solved cubes (position, solution, time),
  • the times you recorded with the timer, with their scramble,
  • your settings (click sounds, haptics, speed) and a count of how many cubes you have solved.

This data lives only in the app’s storage on your device. We have no access to it. If you have set up a backup of your iPhone with Apple (iCloud) or on a computer, it may be part of that backup.

You can delete it at any time: single entries by swiping in the history, everything by deleting the app.

App Store and ratings

The app is distributed through Apple’s App Store. Apple is responsible for the download, for ratings and for the “Enjoying this app?” prompt; Apple’s privacy policy applies. If you have switched on “Share With App Developers” in your iPhone’s settings, Apple provides us with aggregated, anonymous usage and crash statistics. They cannot be traced back to individual people.
